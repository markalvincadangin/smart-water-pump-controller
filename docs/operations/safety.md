# Safety

Safety-related procedures for the Smart Water Pump Controller.

## Pre-energization checklist

Before applying 220V to the pump circuit:

- Complete the **Pre-Energization Checklist** in the project root `README.md` (multimeter, tug test, TOR dial, earth, voltage dividers, etc.).
- Ensure enclosure is properly grounded and all wiring is secure.

## Electrical safety

- **Never** work on live 220V. Isolate and verify dead before touching.
- Follow local electrical codes and use qualified personnel for mains work.
- Dry-run and overflow protection are firmware/dashboard features; they do not replace proper mechanical and electrical safeguards.
