# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful environment for everyone contributing to SmartFlow.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions.
- Focus feedback on code, design, and safety outcomes, not people.
- Assume positive intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Prioritize user safety and reliability in all technical decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome conduct.
- Doxxing, intimidation, or threats.
- Deliberately unsafe guidance for electrical or pump-control procedures.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit/remove comments, close issues/PRs, or block contributors for behavior that violates this policy.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to: markc.dev.iot@gmail.com

Please include relevant links/screenshots and context.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and chat channels related to SmartFlow.
